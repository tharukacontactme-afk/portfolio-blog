Article cover images

Place cover images here named after the article slug (or the cover field in frontmatter).

Recommended for LinkedIn:
  - Size: 1200 x 630 px (or similar 1.91:1 ratio)
  - Format: JPG or PNG

Example files for slug "my-post":
  my-post.jpg
  my-post.png

Optional frontmatter in the .md file:
  cover: my-post

If omitted, the site looks for /images/articles/{slug}.jpg (and other extensions).
